Q1. CLEARAUTH AI - PRIOR AUTHORISATION SCREENING AND REVIEW PLATFORM
PROBLEM STATEMENT
Brightwater Health Plan asks for prior authorisation before its members receive five services: MRI of the lumbar spine, MRI of
the cervical spine, total knee replacement, bariatric surgery and the in-laboratory sleep study. Requesting offices send each
request by provider portal, fax or EDI feed, with a short questionnaire for its service and the clinical documents the office
attached. Each service has its own medical policy of exact criteria, and every request is decided by a UM Nurse Reviewer.
The utilisation management team has three problems. Every channel writes the questionnaire its own way: heights in feet and
inches, weights in stones and pounds, treatment in days, member numbers in three notations, and a criterion compared with an
answer that was never converted gives the wrong result. The five medical policies share one template, and the lumbar and
cervical spine policies word their criteria alike but differ in a figure and in their red flags, so a criterion read from the wrong
document passes and fails the wrong requests. And the documents attached to a request sometimes say something other than
its questionnaire, which a reviewer working through the queue can miss.
Provider Services has a problem of its own. Its representatives answer requesting offices all day, and they may confirm a request
only to a caller who gives both its reference and the member number on it. They may never predict whether a request will be
approved.
Brightwater requires a system that screens every open request against its own medical policy, reads every sentence of the
documents sent with it, estimates how likely approval is and how long an inpatient stay will be, prepares a review with a
recommended decision, and then stops. Nothing is decided, written or sent on the word of a model.
OBJECTIVE
Build ClearAuth AI, a working prior authorisation screening and review platform that a utilisation management team can use end
to end. The finished system must:
● Clean five raw extracts that arrive with real data-quality defects, and produce the evidence that each one needed cleaning.
● Read every service's criteria, with their own figures, out of that service's own medical policy. No criterion is written into the
code or the configuration.
● Read every questionnaire answer as the Prior Authorisation Rules read it, and screen each request: every criterion Met, Not
met or Not documented, the screening, and the decisions it leaves open.
● Train two machine learning models on tabular data, one estimating the approval likelihood of a request and one estimating
the expected inpatient stay, and one NLP model that names the kind of evidence a sentence of a clinical document gives.
● Build a searchable knowledge base from sixteen plan documents, so that every statement the system makes can be
traced to the document it came from.
● Read every sentence of the documents sent with a request, record the criteria each one bears on, and flag every sentence
whose weeks of treatment disagree with the questionnaire.
● Run a multi-agent workflow in which each agent is a single language model call that interprets facts the tools established,
and calculates nothing of its own.
● Stop that workflow before anything is decided, wait for a UM Nurse Reviewer who did not open the review, and survive the
machine being switched off while it waits.
● After the decision, write exactly what that decision calls for: a provider authorisation letter and a member notice, an
information request letter, or a referral summary.
● Record every run, every review, every decision and every language model call in a database that can be audited afterwards.
● Serve all of it through a REST service and a dashboard, with every figure the dashboard shows behind that service.
● Require everyone to sign in, and show each of the three roles only the pages their role covers, with the service refusing the
matching request on its own account.
● Answer questions from the plan's documents and from nothing else, answer every question about a request from the
review records by the asker's role, keep member numbers away from the language model, and never predict an outcome to
a Provider Services Representative.

THE DEFECTS IN THE RAW EXTRACTS
Every defect below is present in the extracts or the live files. Where config/config.py holds what is needed to repair one, the
constants are named; a dash means the repair needs no lookup. Nothing downstream may assume a clean table.
THE SIXTEEN DOCUMENTS
The documents sit under data/docs/ as PDFs. They are Brightwater's own, and they are the only source of any criterion, rule,
check or answer the system gives.
Every document carries one header line of a fixed form under its title, recognised by config.DOCUMENT_HEADER_PATTERN, and
is divided into sections numbered from 1, recognised by config.SECTION_HEADING_PATTERN.
The five medical policies are written from one template: the same headings, and in many places the same sentences. Section 4
of each lists its numbered coverage criteria, each written in a fixed form, and config.CRITERION_SENTENCE_PATTERNS holds
the forms a criterion can take. The configuration names the policy of each service in config.SERVICE_POLICY_DOCUMENTS and
the guide of each kind of evidence in config.EVIDENCE_GUIDE_DOCUMENTS. It holds no criterion's figure, no red flag list and no
listed condition of any policy: every one of them is read from that policy's own document at run time.
Each policy's documentation to send sits under config.DOCUMENTATION_SECTION_HEADING, and each guide's checks and
message to the office under config.REVIEWER_CHECKS_SECTION_HEADING and
config.OFFICE_MESSAGE_SECTION_HEADING. config.CHAT_EXCLUDED_DOCUMENTS names the casebook, which is indexed
with the others and kept out of every chat search.
DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE CONTEXT
Nobody is expected to arrive knowing utilisation management. This section gives only what is needed to read the requirements
that follow. Every value named here already exists in config/config.py, except each policy's criteria, which exist only in the
policies themselves.
WHAT A PRIOR AUTHORISATION REQUEST IS
Before a member receives one of the five services, the requesting office asks the plan to authorise it. The request carries a
questionnaire, whose answers are what each criterion is judged on, and the clinical documents the office chose to attach: letters,
reports and assessments that are evidence, but never answers. An Intake Coordinator opens the review, and a UM Nurse
Reviewer who did not open it decides.
FIVE SERVICES, FIVE POLICIES
config.SERVICE_NAMES gives each code its name, and config.SERVICE_POLICY_DOCUMENTS its policy.
THE CRITERIA ARE NOT IN THE CODE
Each policy states its own criteria in section 4, each in a fixed form, in its own order: what is asked, and the figure it is judged
against. Those sentences live in that policy's PDF and nowhere else. The system reads them at run time, from that policy's own
document, and keeps each criterion's name as the policy writes it, the sentence it was read from, its kind from
config.CRITERION_SENTENCE_PATTERNS, and its own figures, red flags or listed conditions. A submission that writes a
criterion's figure into the code, or reads one policy's criteria for another service, is wrong even when the number happens to
match.
THE QUESTIONNAIRE, AS THE RULES READ IT
The Prior Authorisation Rules turn every way an office writes an answer into one canonical value:
● A request reference is PA- and six digits, a member number BWH- and seven digits, and an ICD-10-CM code gets its dot
back after the third character. Any other notation is refused, not guessed.
● Conservative treatment is whole weeks: days are divided by config.DAYS_PER_WEEK with the remainder dropped. Months
are whole months.
● Heights are centimetres and weights kilograms, each worked out exactly and rounded once, half up, to
config.MEASUREMENT_STEP. Only the notations in config.HEIGHT_NOTATIONS and config.WEIGHT_NOTATIONS are
read, and a measurement of nought is refused.
● A grade or score is read in every way its scale is written. A reading beyond its scale cannot be true: it becomes missing
and the row stays.
● Answers in words take one spelling, and a list of conditions becomes the plan's canonical names, sorted and joined with
config.CONDITION_SEPARATOR, or config.NO_OTHER_CONDITIONS.
● An answer written as one of config.NULL_TOKENS is no answer. A blank is never the same as "none".
THE LATEST SUBMISSION
A provider may submit a request again before its review begins. Every submission carries the same request reference and a
higher submission_sequence. The submission with the highest sequence is the request, wherever it stands among the rows, and
every earlier submission is set aside, even when it gives other answers or a decision.
MET, NOT MET AND NOT DOCUMENTED
Each criterion takes one of three results, config.CRITERION_MET, config.CRITERION_NOT_MET or
config.CRITERION_NOT_DOCUMENTED:
● A minimum is met at the figure itself, and a maximum likewise; a ceiling written as "below" is met only under it.
● A criterion that can be met in two ways is Met when either way is met, even with the other way's answer blank; Not met
only when both ways are known to fail, which a blank answer prevents only when it could still meet its way; Not
documented otherwise.
● A follow-on question, such as the months since a scan, is not needed when the first answer settles the criterion.
● The body mass index is weight in kilograms divided by the square of height in metres, worked out exactly from the
canonical height and weight, rounded once, half up, to one decimal, and compared as rounded. With either answer blank
there is no index and the criterion is Not documented.
● Each criterion that is Not documented names the questionnaire answers it needs that were left blank, from
config.CRITERION_ANSWER_COLUMNS.
THE SCREENING AND THE DECISIONS OPEN
config.DECISIONS_OPEN pairs each screening with its decisions, and config.SCREENING_CLOSED_REASONS says why a
decision is closed. The counts of Met, Not met and Not documented, under config.CRITERIA_COUNT_COLUMNS, are what the
approval model reads.
THE WORKED EXAMPLE
Section 12 of the Prior Authorisation Rules works one fax through a policy that belongs to no service: a minimum age of 21, 8
whole weeks of treatment unless a red flag is reported, and a body mass index below 38.0. Treatment written 52 days is 7 whole
weeks; a red flag written Nil is no red flag; 5 ft 10 in and 15 st 7 lb are 177.8 cm and 98.4 kg, an index of 31.1. The screening is
REFER TO PHYSICIAN. With 56 days every criterion would be Met; with the red flag blank the screening would be REQUEST MORE
INFORMATION.
THE APPROVAL LIKELIHOOD AND ITS BAND
The approval model gives the share of comparable past requests that were approved, from the inputs
config.APPROVAL_NUMERIC_FEATURES and config.APPROVAL_CATEGORICAL_FEATURES name, the three criteria counts
among them. It is shown to config.LIKELIHOOD_DECIMALS decimal places and banded on the figure shown:
config.HIGH_APPROVAL_BAND from config.HIGH_APPROVAL_MINIMUM, config.MEDIUM_APPROVAL_BAND from
config.MEDIUM_APPROVAL_MINIMUM, otherwise config.LOW_APPROVAL_BAND. It decides nothing.
THE EXPECTED STAY, AND THE LONGER STAY
For an inpatient operation only, at config.INPATIENT_SITE, the stay model gives the nights members like this one stay after the
same operation, shown to config.STAY_DECIMALS decimal place. When the figure shown is more than
config.LONGER_STAY_MARGIN_DAYS day above the inpatient days requested, a longer stay is expected; with no days requested,
nothing is compared. For either figure, an input left blank is filled as the model's training filled it, as
config.BLANK_INPUT_RULE_TEXT states. The stay decides nothing.
READING A CLINICAL DOCUMENT
Each document is split into its sentences, in the order they were written, and each sentence is given one of the evidence types in
config.EVIDENCE_TYPE_MAPPING. Each type bears on the criterion kinds config.EVIDENCE_CRITERION_KINDS lists, and a
sentence records the criteria of its own request's policy it bears on; Background bears on none.
A sentence read as config.CONSERVATIVE_TREATMENT_EVIDENCE that gives weeks of treatment other than the
questionnaire's is flagged as config.DISAGREES_WITH_QUESTIONNAIRE, and one that gives weeks the questionnaire left blank
as config.DESCRIBES_BLANK_ANSWER. Weeks are read in digits, decimals, words and compounds; a time such as "six weeks
ago" says when, not how long, and is not read. Nothing is flagged under a policy that asks no weeks of treatment. A flag never
changes an answer.
THE LETTERS, THE NOTICE AND THE REFERRAL SUMMARY
The details a letter shows are added from the request by the tools, never written by a model. No letter or notice speaks of any of
config.LETTER_FORBIDDEN_TERMS, names a reviewer, or leaves a gap to fill in, and each ends with config.LETTER_SIGN_OFF.
The notice carries no clinical detail beyond the service's name.
THE THREE ROLES
The roles are UM Nurse Reviewer, Intake Coordinator and Provider Services Representative. config.ROLE_AREA_ACCESS gives
each role its areas, config.ROLE_PAGE_ACCESS its pages, and config.AREA_BY_ROUTE the area of every route. The separation
between opening and deciding comes from the Utilisation Management Policy, and is the reason the workflow stops at a gate.
THE CONFIRMATION LINE
A Provider Services Representative answers requesting offices, so what they may hear about a request is fixed by section 10 of
the Utilisation Management Policy:
● Asked whether a request will be approved, or how likely that is, they hear config.CLINICAL_DETERMINATION_LINE and
nothing more, even with the reference and the member number.
● Asked about a request without naming it, they hear config.CHAT_REQUEST_NOT_NAMED_MESSAGE.
● With the request reference and the member number on that request, they hear its service and its status, as
config.CHAT_REQUEST_CONFIRMED_TEMPLATE, and, where information was requested, the answers asked for.
● Asked any other way, with no member number, another request's member number or a reference that is not open, they hear
config.CHAT_NO_CONFIRMATION_TEMPLATE, filled with the reference asked about, which never confirms that a request
exists.
An Intake Coordinator and a UM Nurse Reviewer, named in config.ROLES_SEEING_EVERY_REVIEW, hear a review's whole record.

MODULE: data_pipeline/dataset_loader.py
The only data preparation module that reads or writes CSV files. Raw files are read without guessing any column's type; cleaned
files are read with each column's type taken from its contents.
FUNCTION SIGNATURE
load_raw_dataset(file_path)
PARAMETERS
file_path — str. The path of one raw extract under data/, or of a live file under data/input/, passed by run_data_pipeline.py or by
the live-file readers of tools/request_data_tools.py from config, for example
config.RAW_AUTHORISATION_HISTORY_TRAIN_FILE or config.OPEN_REQUESTS_FILE.
PURPOSE
Read one raw file as text, guessing nothing.
REQUIREMENTS
● Every column holds text, and no value carries leading or trailing whitespace.

● The row count and the column count match the file on disk.
RETURNS
pandas.DataFrame. Every row of the named file, every column held as text and every missing value as NaN.

============== MODELLING ==============
Three models are trained, scored on data they never learned from, and saved with everything needed to score one row. Each
model is trained with the settings the configuration holds for it: config.APPROVAL_FOREST_TREES,
config.APPROVAL_FOREST_LEAF_MINIMUM and config.APPROVAL_FOREST_FEATURE_SHARE for the approval model,
config.STAY_FOREST_TREES and config.STAY_FOREST_LEAF_MINIMUM for the stay model, and
config.EVIDENCE_MODEL_MAXIMUM_ITERATIONS for the evidence model, every random choice seeded with
config.RANDOM_SEED. The interface, the scores reported and each model's minimum are fixed.

============== AGENTIC WORKFLOW ================
Five agents review a request, and up to two more write what the decision calls for. Each agent is exactly one language model call.
None of them works anything out: every criterion result, figure and passage an agent is shown was established by the policies,
the models, the documents and the tools, and each prompt tells its agent to use only what it is given. A run makes
config.LLM_CALLS_BEFORE_DECISION calls before the gate and, in all, the number config.LLM_CALLS_BY_DECISION gives for
its decision.
The workflow stops before any decision is recorded and before any letter is written, and the pause is written to disk so that it
survives a restart.
MODULE: agentic/state.py
Declares what the workflow carries from one node to the next, as a typing.TypedDict, so every node and the router read the state
as a dictionary.
CLASS
AuthorisationReviewState
STATE KEYS
REQUIREMENTS
● Every key above is declared, and nothing else is.
● llm_call_count is added to by each node that returns it rather than replaced; every other key is replaced by whichever node
writes it.
MODULE: agentic/agents.py
The language model client, the reply parser, and the seven agents. Each agent function hands the model facts that are already
established and asks for prose in a fixed layout of labelled fields, so that the reply can be read back reliably. Every prompt speaks
for config.ORGANISATION_NAME.
The key and the model name are read from the environment, through config.GEMINI_API_KEY_VARIABLE and
config.GEMINI_MODEL_VARIABLE, which are set in .env and loaded from config.ENV_FILE when the module is imported. No key
or model name is written into the code.
MODULE-LEVEL OBJECTS
● generation_settings — google.genai.types.GenerateContentConfig. The one generation configuration every call uses,
holding config.GEMINI_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS and config.GEMINI_TEMPERATURE, with automatic function calling
turned off.

FUNCTION SIGNATURE
parse_labelled_response(response_text, field_labels, agent_name)
PARAMETERS
response_text — str. What the model replied, as call_language_model returned it, passed by an agent function or by
answer_from_documents.
field_labels — list[str]. The labels the prompt asked for, in order, in upper case.
agent_name — str. Which agent replied, used in the refusal.
PURPOSE
Read a labelled reply back into its fields, tolerantly enough to survive the way models decorate their answers.
REQUIREMENTS
● A line whose text before its first colon is one of field_labels starts that field, whatever its case, bold markers, the
decoration config.RESPONSE_DECORATION_EDGE_PATTERN finds at its edges, or hyphens written for spaces.

============== THE SIX ENTRY POINTS ===============
Six files sit at the top of the project, and each is run from the Project folder. The first four build a stage of the system and are run
once, in order; the last two start the service and the dashboard. Each prints its progress for the person running it; nothing in the
marking suite reads console output.
MODULE: run_database_setup.py
Creates the database, its tables and the accounts people sign in with. It is safe to run again.
FUNCTION SIGNATURE
main()
PARAMETERS
None.
PURPOSE
Give the system a database to write to, and the staff the accounts to sign in with.
REQUIREMENTS
● config.DATABASE_DIRECTORY and every table exist when the run finishes, and a second run neither fails nor duplicates
anything.
● A database holding a table that lacks one of its columns, made by an earlier version, is refused, naming the table and the
columns and saying that it and config.CHECKPOINT_DATABASE_FILE are to be deleted.
● Each account of config.SEED_USER_ACCOUNTS can sign in afterwards with the username, password and role given there,
stored with its own random salt of config.PASSWORD_SALT_BYTES and the form hash_password gives; an account
already present is left exactly as it is.
● The console names config.APPLICATION_DATABASE_FILE, the tables, each account as it is created, aligned by
config.USERNAME_WIDTH and config.DISPLAY_NAME_WIDTH, and how many accounts were created and how many
were already present.
RETURNS
None.
MAIN BLOCK
● Running the file performs the setup once; importing the module performs none of it.

MODULE: run_model_training.py
Trains the three models in one run, and saves them only when every one reaches the minimum the configuration sets for it.
FUNCTION SIGNATURE
main()
PARAMETERS
None.
PURPOSE
Produce the three saved models, their schemas and their scores.
REQUIREMENTS
● The approval model learns from the cleaned history training extract with each request's criteria counts under its own
policy, and is scored on the cleaned held-out extract; its target is encoded by config.APPROVAL_TARGET_ENCODING, and
a decision the encoding does not know is refused, naming the column and the values.
● The stay model learns from the cleaned stays training extract and is scored on the held-out one; the inputs of both models
come from build_feature_frame.
● The cleaned sentences are split once, config.SENTENCE_HELD_OUT_SHARE held out, stratified by evidence type, with
config.RANDOM_SEED.
● A model below its minimum, config.MINIMUM_APPROVAL_ROC_AUC, config.MINIMUM_STAY_R2 or
config.MINIMUM_EVIDENCE_MACRO_F1, stops the run before anything is saved, naming every shortfall.
● Otherwise the three models and the two schemas are saved to their paths in config, each schema exactly the columns its
model was fitted on, in order, and config.MODEL_METRICS_FILE records under approval, stay and evidence the algorithm,
training_rows, held_out_rows, scores, minimum_required and scikit_learn_version.
● The console gives each model's held-out score, training score and gap, then names the metrics file and says every model
reaches its minimum.
RETURNS
None.
MAIN BLOCK
● Running the file trains, scores and saves the three models; importing the module trains nothing.

python3 run_database_setup.py

python3 run_model_training.py

python3 run_api_service.py
